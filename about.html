What I do

I help make product information easier to see and understand.

A product listing needs more than attractive graphics. Shoppers should be able to quickly understand the product, its main benefits, important details and how it fits their needs. My focus is creating organized visuals that support that experience.

I work on new Amazon image sets and on updates to existing listing visuals, A+ Content, Premium A+, Storefronts and Brand Story assets.

My strengths

  • Amazon-specific service focus
  • Clear product benefit presentation
  • Consistent layouts across a listing
  • Willingness to revise designs based on agreed feedback
  • Direct, straightforward communication
My commitment

Understand the product before designing.

I start by reviewing the listing and available product information, then focus the design on what shoppers need to notice and understand. I aim for clean layouts, readable text and a consistent visual direction.
