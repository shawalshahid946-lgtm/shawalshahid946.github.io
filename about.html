My background

Design experience built around Amazon listings

Over the past two years, I have developed experience working on Amazon-focused visual assets. I understand that each image needs a clear purpose: show the product accurately, explain its strongest features, answer buyer questions and keep the brand presentation consistent from the product gallery to the detail page.

My office experience has helped me learn how to work within project requirements, follow a brief, handle feedback, keep files organized and prepare deliverables carefully. I am currently working in an office, where I continue to apply and improve my design skills.

I work on both new creative assets and updates to existing Amazon content. If a listing already has images, I can help refresh the visual hierarchy, improve readability, organize feature callouts and bring the set closer to the brand's visual style.

I use Adobe Photoshop and Canva. My approach is practical and product-focused: start with the product information, identify the main buyer benefits, then turn those details into a clean visual story.
