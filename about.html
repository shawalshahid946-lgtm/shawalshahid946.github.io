About me

Hi, I’m Shawal Shahid.

I’m an Amazon Graphic Designer with two years of experience creating and updating product visuals for Amazon listings. My focus is helping brands present their products clearly through purposeful design—not just decoration.

My work

Amazon content from creation to update

I work on listing image sets, product infographics, A+ Content, Premium A+, Amazon Storefronts and Brand Story visuals. I can help plan new creative assets or refresh existing designs based on the product information and brand guidelines you provide.

I use Adobe Photoshop and Canva in my workflow. I aim to keep the design hierarchy clear, product details readable and the visual style consistent across all assets.

My process starts with understanding the product, target customer, key features and project requirements. Then I confirm the deliverables and timeline before designing.
