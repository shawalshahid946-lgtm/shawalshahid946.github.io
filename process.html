My Process

Simple steps. Clear expectations.

My workflow is designed to make the project easy to understand before design begins. We confirm the product, deliverables, timeline, price and revision scope first.

01

Share the product link

Send the Amazon listing link or ASIN, product category, target marketplace and a short description of what you need.

02

Discuss the scope

We confirm image count, A+ module count, Storefront pages, source assets, timeline, price and revisions.

03

Design the content

I create the agreed visuals using your product information, assets and brand guidelines.

04

Review and revise

You review the draft and share consolidated feedback. Included revisions follow the agreed quote.

05

Final delivery

I prepare the final files in the agreed format and organize them for your team to upload.

06

Optional next step

If you later need more listing images, A+ modules or Storefront updates, we can scope that as a new project.

Frequently asked questions

10 common questions

If your project has a special requirement, contact me and we can discuss it before work begins.

1. What services do you offer?

Amazon Listing Images, Product Infographics, Lifestyle Images, Standard A+ Content / EBC, Premium A+, Brand Story, Storefront design and existing content updates.

2. Do you create new designs and update existing ones?

Yes. I can create new creative assets or refresh existing images and modules based on the product information and brand guidelines you provide.

3. What do you need to start?

Please send the Amazon listing link or ASIN, required deliverables, product images, brand assets and any important features or claims to highlight.

4. How much do you charge?

My pricing page lists starting rates. The final quote depends on scope, image or module count, complexity, available assets, deadline and revisions.

5. How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the deliverables and how quickly feedback and product information are provided. I confirm the expected timeline before starting.

6. How many revisions are included?

The listed packages generally include two revision rounds. Additional revisions or a changed brief may need a separate quote.

7. Can you design Premium A+?

I can design Premium A+ creative assets. Publishing availability depends on your Amazon account, brand eligibility and current Amazon requirements.

8. Do you upload designs to Seller Central?

The standard quote covers design files ready for upload. Seller Central upload or implementation can be discussed separately if you need it.

9. Which tools do you use?

I use Adobe Photoshop and Canva for Amazon-focused product visuals.

10. How can I contact you and request a quote?

Send your Amazon listing link and project requirements through WhatsApp or email. I will review the scope and confirm a quote before work begins.
