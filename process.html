My process

From product link to finished creative assets

A simple process helps us agree on what is being designed, what information is needed and when each stage will be delivered.

01 — Share your listing

Send the Amazon product URL, marketplace and a short description of what you want to improve or create.

02 — Confirm the scope

We agree on the number and type of images/modules/pages, brand guidelines, copy, timeline, price and revision rounds.

03 — Gather product details

You provide accurate product features, dimensions, approved claims, product photos, brand assets and any reference examples.

04 — Design and review

I prepare the agreed visuals for your review. You share consolidated feedback so revisions can be handled efficiently.

05 — Final delivery

After approval and payment according to our agreement, I deliver the agreed final files in the specified format.

Amazon requirements, image rules, Premium A+ eligibility and available Storefront modules can vary. The seller is responsible for confirming account eligibility and approving factual product claims before publishing.
